== Abstract Summary ==
This extraction prompt guides an AI to convert photocatalytic‐CO₂-reduction PDF articles into a compact MediaWiki entry.  All content must derive explicitly from the paper; unverifiable guesses are forbidden.  Numerical fields are captured in standardized units (µM, mM, M, °C, nm, h, h⁻¹, %, mW cm⁻²) to allow direct database comparison.  Every distinct experimental condition becomes one CSV row, ensuring maximum recall.  Where the article is silent or ambiguous the extractor writes “not reported”, never leaving prose blanks.  Strict syntax, unit, and column-order rules below guarantee machine-readable output while minimising post-processing.

== Advances and Special Progress ==
The prompt emphasises capturing differentiating advances: increased turnover numbers, higher CO selectivity, earth-abundant components, unusual photosensitiser–catalyst coupling, solvent tolerance, mechanistic insight, or durability improvements.  Such details must come from the article’s own claims, control experiments, or comparative tables.  Inflated or speculative language is disallowed.  Stated advances belong in this section only if at least one concrete experimental metric in the CSV supports them, preserving alignment between narrative and data.

Note structural variants tested, decomposition pathways, and performance highlights strictly as reported.  Avoid over-long ligand descriptions; prefer the paper’s shorthand.  Concentrations appear only in the CSV, not here.  Stay under the word limit.
